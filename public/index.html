What it does

Matches by meaning
“Postgres” and PostgreSQL are the same skill. “Built REST services in Flask” counts toward both Python and REST.
Ranks the pile
Drop in a folder of resumes and every one is scored against your job description and sorted by fit.
Hides identity
Surname, email, phone, links and photos come off. Download a watermarked PDF to share.
Reads the JD for you
Upload or paste it and the requirements fill themselves in. Anything missing is flagged.
One desk per recruiter
Your job descriptions, candidates and settings are private to your account.
Deletes on request
Remove a job description or a candidate whenever you want. Full data removal is handled by your administrator.

How it works

  1. Add the job description.Requirements are extracted for you to confirm.
  2. Drop in resumes.PDF, DOCX or TXT. Each one comes back scored, with skills matched and missing.
  3. Share the shortlist.Mask personal details, add your watermark, send.

Fewer resumes to read.
